Thaurus Guru — Sales Intelligence OS

An AI-powered sales intelligence platform built to support Thaurus Guru Sales Executives.

The platform combines Google's Gemini AI with a secure backend, persistent database and adaptive learning system.

Core capabilities

- Live Sales Assistant: Generate natural responses to customer messages and questions.
- Objection Intelligence: Handle customer resistance with strategic, evidence-based responses.
- Closing Intelligence: Identify buying signals and recommend effective closing approaches.
- Cold Call Studio: Develop engaging introductions, discovery questions and call strategies.
- Product Intelligence: Access verified Thaurus Guru products, pricing, trading rules and payout information.
- Prospect Intelligence: Analyse customer conversations, motivations and potential buying signals.
- Sales Simulation: Practise realistic sales conversations with challenging AI prospects.
- Performance Analytics: Track sales activities, identify weaknesses and monitor improvement.
- Adaptive Learning: Learn from feedback, conversation history and recorded sales outcomes.

Technology

- Frontend: React and TypeScript.
- Backend: Node.js and TypeScript.
- Database: Firebase Firestore.
- Authentication: Firebase Authentication.
- AI engine: Google Gemini API.
- Storage: Firebase Storage.

Core objective

Build an intelligent sales partner that responds quickly during live customer interactions, analyses complicated sales situations deeply, remembers useful experiences and continuously improves its recommendations through verified knowledge and feedback.
